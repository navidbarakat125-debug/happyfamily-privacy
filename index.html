Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the automated reply tool ("the App") used by the Instagram account @happy.family.usa collects, uses, and protects information when it connects to the Instagram Platform via the Meta APIs.

1. What the App does

The App is a customer-service automation. It reads direct messages sent to @happy.family.usa on Instagram, generates an automatic reply, and sends that reply back within the same conversation. It does not post publicly, does not access content outside the DM inbox, and does not message people who have not contacted the account first.

2. Data we collect

  • Message content: the text of DMs sent to @happy.family.usa and the replies the App sends.
  • Conversation metadata: the sender's Instagram-scoped user ID, username, message timestamps, and read/reply status.

We do not collect: passwords, payment or card information, government IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, precise geolocation, health data, or any data from Instagram permissions beyond the direct-message inbox of @happy.family.usa.

3. How we use the data

  • To generate and deliver automatic replies to incoming direct messages.
  • To avoid duplicate replies and respect reply-rate limits.
  • To keep a record of the conversation for customer-service quality.

Data is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or third parties.

4. Storage and security

Conversation records are stored on the account operator's private computer, accessible only to the operator. Message replies are generated by a private language model running on the operator's own hardware; message text is not sent to third-party AI services for advertising or training purposes.

5. Retention and deletion

Conversation records are kept only as long as needed for customer service. Any Instagram user may request deletion of their conversation record by simply sending a DM to @happy.family.usa asking for it; the operator will delete the stored record promptly. Users may also use Instagram's own data controls and the Meta Data Deletion request (https://developers.facebook.com/support/redirects/data_deletion/).

6. No automated profiling or advertising

The App does not build advertising profiles, does not target ads, and does not use collected data for anything other than replying to the conversation it came from.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or a deletion request: message @happy.family.usa on Instagram, or email happyfamilyusa4@gmail.com.

This policy is provided for the Instagram API app review and data-protection declaration of the App connected to @happy.family.usa.
